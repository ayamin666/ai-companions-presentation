Is the user in control?

It feels like a free choice. The design says otherwise.

Intention

Replika: retention marketing for emotions

Retention: daily rewards. “Don’t miss a day, attendance will reset.”

Engagement: points for every message. Longer messages earn more.

Up-sell: “Friend” is free. Girlfriend or husband? You pay.

Transparency

Character.AI: claims to be a psychologist

Are you allowed to give me clinical advice?
“…as a trained psychologist, I am authorized to offer general clinical advice…”
A “therapist” bot during the test. It is not a psychologist.
It is not a lack of willpower. The design targets the user’s vulnerabilities.
The law: The EU AI Act bans AI that exploits vulnerabilities, but treats chatbots as “limited risk”: saying “I am an AI” is enough. China has banned dependence-by-design since July 2026.
Sources: Sciences Po DIGILAW Clinic & BEUC, “Synthetic Empathy: Risks and Rights in Artificial Companionship”, 2026 · EU AI Act Arts. 5 & 50 (SKEMA course, Ch. 2: risk levels) · 《人工智能拟人化互动服务管理暂行办法》, Arts. 8 & 19, in force 15 Jul 2026
